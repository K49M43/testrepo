## Financial target representation
Let \(W_{t,\mathrm{gross}}\) be portfolio wealth at date \(t\) before investor taxes, \(T_t\) accurately estimated **tax liabilities on the modeled liquidation/withdrawal policy** (including taxable distributions paid earlier), \(C_t\) applicable unrecovered costs, and \(\Pi_{2026\to t}\) the correctly linked inflation factor.

\[
W^{\mathrm{real,aftertax}}_t = (W_{t,\mathrm{gross}} - T_t - C_t)\, /\, \Pi_{2026\to t}
\]

Track taxes paid from the portfolio or outside the portfolio explicitly; do not subtract twice. For a rolling exit, calculate after-tax accessible wealth under the specified liquidation schedule; do not assume all unrealized gains are taxed on a forced October exit.

Report these in the same order in EVERY decision report:
1. Nominal initial capital, exact SIP schedule, gross nominal terminal value and gains;
2. Costs, tax by instrument/lot/event, net nominal accessible value;
3. Inflation factor and net real 2026-purchasing-power value;
4. \(P(W^{\mathrm{real,aftertax}} \ge ₹90L)\), \(P(W^{\mathrm{real,aftertax}} \ge ₹1Cr)\), shortfall severity and confidence/uncertainty interval **only if model qualified**;
5. Money-weighted IRR/XIRR with dated contributions and withdrawals, plus the required hurdle under explicit assumptions.

A **10-year 6% inflation factor is ~1.79085**, so ₹90L and ₹1Cr in 2026 purchasing power correspond to about **₹1.612 crore** and **₹1.791 crore** in 2036 after-tax nominal wealth **only if exactly ten years and constant 6% inflation**. These are illustrative translation checks, not guaranteed future amounts.

- Do not substitute a 3-year ETF track record for a 10-year realized silver fund history; use a documented two-layer benchmark/product model where necessary.
