I am an individual investor conducting personal, non-commercial research to evaluate a long-horizon investment decision. I am not requesting account-specific, member-only, or confidential data. Please confirm whether an individual investor is eligible to request data as a non-commercial user under CCIL's Data Sharing Policy and identify the current formal request/undertaking process, any applicable fee, and the authorized submission channel.

Please confirm the price basis (clean/dirty), yield convention, date convention (trade/settlement), treatment of cancellations/reversals, and whether the report covers all trades reported for settlement or a defined subset. ## Process and controls

1. Investor reviews and edits this draft; confirm the non-commercial purpose and whether individual-investor eligibility is established.
2. If the investor explicitly authorizes contact, submit CCIL's formal request form through its verified official channel, with only necessary identity details. Do not attach account statements, broker credentials or unnecessary personal financial records.
3. Review CCIL's response, fee and undertaking before accepting. Confirm private retention period and whether derived outputs can be disclosed publicly.
4. If approved, acquire only the permitted date range/fields through the designated route; preserve source bytes, timestamp, hash, schema, missing dates and corrections privately.
5. Keep raw data and any restricted derived data out of the public repository unless the written undertaking specifically permits publication. If terms do not support the required research and disclosure, record the source as unavailable and use only independently licensed evidence.

## Basis

CCIL's published Data Sharing Policy describes non-commercial data requests as research/internal-analysis requests, requires a formal request and undertaking, prohibits re-dissemination/re-distribution, and states that ownership remains with CCIL. The public website says commercial use requires written permission. This draft does not interpret eligibility or grant any permission. See the [CCIL Data Sharing Policy](https://www.ccilindia.com/documents/d/ccil/ccil_datasharing_policy_oct25) and [Volumewise Outright Trade report](https://www.ccilindia.com/volumewise-outright-trade).
