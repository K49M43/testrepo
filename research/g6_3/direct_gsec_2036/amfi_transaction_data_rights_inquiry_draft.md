# Draft inquiry — AMFI debt transaction data use rights

**Status:** Draft only. Not sent. No personal or account information included.

**To:** AMFI contact channel listed on its official website  
**Subject:** Permission clarification for limited personal investment research using debt transaction disclosures

Dear AMFI team,

I am conducting non-commercial research to evaluate a Government of India security for my own long-term investment decision. AMFI's public "Transaction in Debt and Money Market Instruments" page provides transaction fields searchable by ISIN and an Excel export. I have reviewed the page and its Terms of Use, which permit personal, non-commercial access but restrict publication and electronic storage of significant portions of site content.

Could you please clarify whether AMFI would permit the following narrowly scoped use for one ISIN and a defined post-issue date range:

1. Download the filtered Excel output for private, non-commercial analysis and retain the source file locally with its retrieval date and cryptographic hash.
2. Reconcile transaction price, accrued interest, quantity, date, and trade-type fields for reproducibility and internal investment-decision analysis.
3. Publish a small derived summary in a public personal research repository, without raw rows, fund-house or scheme names, or investor information. The derived output would be limited to observation counts, date coverage and gaps, aggregate trade-volume ranges, and documented validation results.
4. Cite and link the AMFI disclosure page and include any attribution, retention period, or disclaimer you require.

If any of these uses are not permitted, please specify which uses require a separate licence or written agreement, whether a personal-research licence is available, and whether derived security-level aggregates may be published. I will not download, retain, automate, or publish the transaction data unless the applicable permission is clear.

Thank you,

Investor

---

## Handling note

Do not send without the investor's explicit authorization. Current AMFI access is not qualified for public-repository evidence. The repository should record the response, applicable scope, date, and any licence terms before any data is retrieved or incorporated.
