# RBI enquiry on a 91-day Treasury bill holiday payment

**Draft prepared on 25 November 2026**

**To** `pdomumbai@rbi.org.in`

**Cc** `support@rbiretaildirect.org.in`

**Subject** Redemption payment date and Retail Direct credit for T-bill IN002024X086

Dear Public Debt Office and RBI Retail Direct team,

I am checking the redemption and reinvestment timing for Government of India 91-day Treasury bills held through RBI Retail Direct. My example is ISIN IN002024X086, which NSE circular CML62065 lists with a maturity date of 15 August 2024.

1. Could the Public Debt Office confirm the actual date on which RBI released the redemption payment for this ISIN and share the applicable holiday payment circular, rule or dated record? Please identify whether the rule applies to Treasury bills held through Retail Direct and which business-day calendar it uses.
2. For this ISIN, when were redemption funds sent to the linked bank accounts of Retail Direct holders? If records from 2024 are unavailable, please share the published processing window, including whether the time funds become available depends on the receiving bank.
3. What was the Retail Direct funding deadline in IST for the 14 August 2024 T-bill auction? Could proceeds for this ISIN have been available before that deadline, and is such availability governed by a published procedure?

An official circular reference or a non-personal extract of the relevant payment record would be sufficient. I am enquiring about the instrument and process and do not need any holder or bank account details.

Thank you.

## Source notes for the project

- [NSE CML62065](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/CML62065.pdf), page 2, lists IN002024X086 with a maturity date of 15 August 2024. [NSE CML63038](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/CML63038.pdf), page 1, records a 12 August trading suspension. The archived 14 August RBI auction notice (press release 58465) gives 16 August as settlement. The Retail Direct funding question concerns the bid cutoff, which precedes settlement.
- [Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, section 25](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15327/1/negotiable_instruments_act%2C_1881.pdf) deems a promissory note or bill of exchange due on the preceding business day when its maturity falls on a public holiday. Paragraph 7 of the retained T-bill notification provides for a promissory note or an SGL credit. [RBI's Government Securities Act FAQ, questions 1 and 9](https://www.rbi.org.in/CommonPerson/english/Scripts/FAQs.aspx?Id=380) distinguishes Government Promissory Notes from stock held in a Subsidiary General Ledger account. The legal provision supports investigating the preceding day, with applicability to the relevant Retail Direct holding and the actual payment still open.
- [RBI Retail Direct FAQ, questions 18 and 33](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/FS_FAQs.aspx?Id=145) identifies the linked savings account as the redemption destination and requires auction bids to be funded before the bidding window closes. The repayment due date, RBI release, bank availability and bid deadline need their own evidence.
- Recipient addresses come from [RBI Mumbai Departments](https://www.rbi.org.in/Regionalbranch/Mumbai/depts.aspx) and [RBI Retail Direct FAQ, question 50](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/FS_FAQs.aspx?Id=145). If the enquiry yields no usable record, [RBI's Right to Information page](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/Righttoinfoact.aspx) identifies the route for requesting a copy of an existing record.

**Acceptance** A first-party record can qualify the payment date for this ISIN. A broader rule requires confirmation of scope and the governing calendar before application to other securities. A same-day funding case requires both a time when the bank made the funds available and the historical Retail Direct funding cutoff. Current evidence supports a conditional rollover scenario with same-day proceeds excluded.
